Our Artists

The voices and talents behind Monarch-Rise Music

Lilongwe Collective

Afro-fusion · Pop

A dynamic group of musicians from Malawi's capital, blending traditional rhythms with modern production. Their sound is a celebration of urban African culture.

Malaŵi Sunrise Blantyre Nights Lake Malawi

Nyasa Rhythms

Traditional · Folk

Preserving and reimagining Malawi's rich musical heritage. Nyasa Rhythms brings the sounds of the lake to a new generation with authentic instrumentation.

Lake Malawi (Live) Malawi Freedom Mulanje Mountain

Mzuzu Vibes

Urban · Hip-Hop

From the northern city of Mzuzu, this collective brings raw energy and street-smart lyricism to the Malawian music scene. Their sound is fresh, bold, and unapologetic.

Zomba Groove Northern Lights Mzuzu City

Blantyre Soul

R&B · Soul

Smooth, soulful, and deeply emotional. Blantyre Soul brings a contemporary R&B sound with authentic Malawian storytelling that resonates with listeners everywhere.

Blantyre Nights Soul of Malawi City Lights
