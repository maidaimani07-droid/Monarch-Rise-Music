About Us

Where Talent Rises. Where Music Reigns.

Our Story

From Malawi to the World

Monarch-Rise Music was born from a simple belief: that Malawi's music deserves a global stage. Founded in 2024, our label has grown from a small collective of passionate artists into a movement that champions the rich musical heritage of our nation.

We believe in the power of talent, the magic of creativity, and the importance of preserving and promoting Malawi's unique sound. Our artists are more than musicians—they are storytellers, cultural ambassadors, and the voices of a new generation.

Our Mission
